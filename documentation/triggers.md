# Business Rules - Triggers

## Business Requirement

Gochelicious required customer support requests to be automatically routed to the appropriate teams based on the nature of each enquiry while reducing manual ticket assignment.

## Configuration

Configured Zendesk triggers to automatically assign tickets to specialized support groups, apply ticket tags, prioritize VIP customers, and acknowledge newly created support requests.

## Business Value

The trigger configuration improves operational efficiency by automating ticket routing, ensuring consistent categorization, reducing response times, and enhancing the customer support experience.
