# Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

## Business Requirement

To improve customer support operations, service level agreements (SLAs) were configured based on customer tiers. Premium customers receive faster response targets while standard customers follow defined service expectations, ensuring consistent support delivery across the organization.

## SLA Policies Configured

- VIP Customer SLA
- Corporate Client SLA
- Standard Customer SLA
- Gold Customer SLA

## Service Targets

| SLA Policy | Business Purpose |
|------------|------------------|
| VIP Customer SLA | Provides the fastest response times for Platinum customers requiring priority support. |
| Corporate Client SLA | Ensures business clients receive accelerated responses for commercial enquiries. |
| Standard Customer SLA | Defines the default response targets for everyday customer support requests. |
| Gold Customer SLA | Provides enhanced service levels for Gold-tier customers between Standard and VIP support. |

## SLA Metrics Configured

Each SLA policy includes measurable service objectives for:

- First Reply Time
- Next Reply Time
- Requester Wait Time
- Agent Work Time

Priority-based response targets were configured for:

- Urgent
- High
- Normal
- Low

## Business Value

The SLA framework establishes measurable response expectations, prioritizes premium customers, improves operational accountability, and enables support performance to be monitored against defined service targets.
