# Business Rules – Automations

## Business Requirement

Gochelicious required automated workflows to improve ticket management, reduce manual follow-up, and ensure timely customer service.

## Automations Configured

- Automatically closes solved tickets after four days.
- Sends reminders for tickets awaiting customer responses.
- Escalates VIP customer requests based on priority and age.
- Flags shipping-related tickets for follow-up.
- Flags return requests for follow-up.

## Business Value

These automations reduce manual work, improve ticket visibility, support service-level objectives, and help ensure customers receive timely responses throughout the support lifecycle.
