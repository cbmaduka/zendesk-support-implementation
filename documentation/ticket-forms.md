# Ticket Forms

## Business Requirement

Gochelicious required a standardized customer support form to ensure agents consistently collected the information necessary to resolve enquiries related to orders, returns, payments, and product issues.

## Configuration

A custom ticket form was created to capture essential customer and order information, including order details, product attributes, payment method, shipping carrier, customer tier, and preferred communication channel.

## Business Value

The standardized ticket form improves data consistency, reduces follow-up requests, accelerates ticket resolution, and supports workflow automation and reporting.
